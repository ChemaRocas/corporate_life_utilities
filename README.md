# corporate_life_utilities
Small functions/codes that I have found helpful in my corporate life; mainly to substitute depending on webpages that keep your data or for liberating yourself from annoying and repetitive tasks. Disclaimer: most of this is vibe coded with Claude.

## Tools

- [pdf_unlocker](pdf_unlocker/): removes passwords and copy/edit restrictions from every PDF in a folder, locally on your computer, keeping your highlights and drawings.
- [background_remover](background_remover/): removes the background from an image or a whole folder of images and saves transparent PNGs at the original resolution, locally and offline.
